# Launch Video Storyboard

Most launch videos fail in the first 2 seconds. Structure everything around **hook → problem → reveal → proof → call to action**.

## 1. Brief

Product, audience, the ONE message, primary platform (X/LinkedIn 16:9 or 1:1, TikTok/Reels/Shorts 9:16, website hero), length (default 30s), tone (calm premium / energetic / playful / technical), available assets (screen recordings, logo, brand colors, founder on camera?).

## 2. Structure (30s default; scale proportionally)

| Beat | Time | Purpose | Rules |
|------|------|---------|-------|
| Hook | 0–2s | Stop the scroll | Show the payoff or a bold visual first; big 3–5 word text |
| Problem | 2–7s | Make it relatable | One pain, shown not told |
| Reveal | 7–12s | Introduce the product | Logo + product name + one-line promise |
| Show | 12–24s | 2–3 key moments | Real UI in motion; one feature per beat; zoom into what matters |
| Proof | 24–27s | Build trust | One number, quote or logo row |
| CTA | 27–30s | Tell them what to do | URL/button + offer; hold 2s |

## 3. Deliverables

1. **Script**: voiceover lines with timestamps (≈ 2.5 words per second), plus on-screen text (≤ 6 words per card).
2. **Storyboard table**: `# | Time | Visual | Camera/motion | On-screen text | VO | SFX/music`.
3. **Screen-recording shot list**: exact UI flows to record, at 2× resolution, with cursor hidden or smoothed, dummy data prepared, and zoom targets noted.
4. **AI B-roll prompts**: for any non-UI shots, write model-ready prompts using the 8-part shot formula (shot type, subject, action, setting, camera, lens/look, lighting, audio) with aspect ratio and duration.
5. **Motion notes**: text animation style (e.g. mask-reveal, 400ms, ease-out), transitions (match cut, whip, zoom-through UI), and brand color and font usage.
6. **Audio**: music mood and BPM range (e.g. 100–120 BPM upbeat electronic), 3–5 SFX cues (whoosh, click, pop), and a VO direction (pace, warmth).
7. **Cut-downs**: a 15s version and a 6s bumper; safe-zone notes for 9:16 (keep text away from top 14% and bottom 20%).
8. **Captions**: burned-in caption style and an .srt file for the VO.

## 4. Optional: animated storyboard preview

If asked, build a single `storyboard.html` that plays the beats as timed CSS/JS scenes (text cards, UI placeholders, transitions) at the chosen aspect ratio, with a play/pause button and a timeline scrubber, so the team can feel the pacing before production.

## Quality checklist

- Is the product visible by second 7?
- Can someone understand the video with sound off?
- One idea per beat, one message overall?
- Does the CTA say exactly what to do and where?
- Are all visuals original or licensed (no copyrighted music, footage, logos or characters)?
