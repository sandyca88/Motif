## Usage rules
- Accent ≤ 10% of any screen; accent-2 only for data viz or one highlight per viewport.
- On dark palettes, use 1px borders at rgba(255,255,255,.08) instead of shadows.
- Re-check contrast whenever text sits on accent (e.g. button labels).
