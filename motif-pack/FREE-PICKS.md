# Free Picks: open-source design skills we recommend

A curated "Free picks" list for your site. **Link to the original repos and credit the authors. Don't re-host these or bundle them into paid plans.** MIT and Apache licenses technically allow redistribution, but reselling someone else's free skill damages trust fast in this community. Linking to them builds it.

Licenses were checked on Sep 30, 2026. Re-check before you publish.

## Don't list as redistributable

- Anthropic's docx/pdf/pptx/xlsx skills are source-available, not open source.
- Skillry and MotionSites items: their terms prohibit redistribution.

## How to show them on your site

Use a "Free picks" row with plain link cards: name, author, license badge, one line on why, and "Pairs with Motif ___". Don't use their screenshots or logos as your thumbnails. A simple text card, or a generic icon, keeps it clearly theirs.
